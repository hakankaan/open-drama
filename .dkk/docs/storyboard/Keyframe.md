## Summary

A still of a shot's opening moment, drawn from its characters, scene and props before any video is paid for. The studio shows every keyframe of the episode side by side to compare faces, outfits and light; on a video model that takes a first frame the shot's video starts from it, and the film's colour match aims each clip at it.
