# storyboard

> Auto-generated — do not edit manually. Run `domain-knowledge-kit render` to regenerate.

The breakdown of an episode's script into shots — each shot is one video-generation unit of 8-15 seconds carrying 2-4 sub-shots — plus per-shot video prompts, reference bindings to characters, scenes and props, and the generated shot videos.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition | Aliases |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Sub-shot** | One visual unit of two to six seconds inside a shot, marked as a [Shot N] block in the description. A shot may switch framing between its sub-shots, but all of them happen in the same scene. | — |
| **Video prompt** | The generation prompt for a shot — one untimed Shot N line per sub-shot, each with one camera move, a mention on every appearance and dialogue in braces after its speaker. Assets are referenced with the delimited mention grammar @[Name] (brackets allow multi-word names); on generation the API turns each mention into an ordered reference-image slot, the video adapter renders it in the provider's own token syntax, and the API adds the style, a slot legend, the scene's lighting and the identity, clean-frame and dialogue-language sentences (adr-0017). | — |
| **Keyframe** | A still of a shot's opening moment, drawn from its characters, scene and props before any video is paid for. The studio shows every keyframe of the episode side by side to compare faces, outfits and light; on a video model that takes a first frame the shot's video starts from it, and the film's colour match aims each clip at it. | — |
| **Continuous take** | Shots joined without a cut: a shot marked as continuing the previous one starts its video on that shot's last frame, so the action carries on, and the merge joins the two clips on that frame. At most two shots in a row continue the one before, since they see no reference image and faces drift. | — |
| **Line** | What a shot's one speaker says, as spoken, without quotes or stage directions. Its line audio is the line read by a speech model in the speaker's voice (adr-0021). | — |
| **Pinned voices** | A drama setting under which every line is spoken by a speech model in its character's voice and the shot's video is made silent and lip-synced to it, so a character sounds the same in every shot (adr-0021). | — |
| **Reference binding** | The characters, scene and props whose reference images are sent with the shot's video request, at most four characters and five assets per call (adr-0017); which views of each character go depends on the video model (adr-0018). Extra reference images, videos or audio can be uploaded per shot. | — |
| **Breakdown** | The storyboard-breaker agent run that replaces the episode's shots from the current script, saving in batches of at most 8 with idempotent upsert by shot number. | — |
| **Beat** | A story unit such as the setup, the inciting moment, the climax or a reversal. Two beats never share a shot, so a beat change is always a place where one shot ends and the next begins. | — |
| **Storyboard segment** | What the Shot aggregate represents — one 8-15 second video-generation unit carrying 2-4 sub-shots. | Segment |
