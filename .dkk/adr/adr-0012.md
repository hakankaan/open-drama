# ADR-0012 — Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

## Context

Open Drama is published as an open, self-hostable tool. The product owner wants personal, educational and non-commercial use to be free, modifications to stay open, and commercial use to require permission. Every file is original work (`adr-0001`), so the licence applies to the whole repository.

## Decision

- The repository is licensed **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International** (`LICENSE` at the root, SPDX `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0` in every `package.json`).
- Commercial use is not permitted without the copyright holders' permission, and derivative works must keep this licence.

## Alternatives Considered

- **MIT or Apache-2.0.** Rejected by the product owner: they allow commercial use without permission.
- **AGPL-3.0.** Not chosen: it keeps modifications open, including for network use, but does not restrict commercial use.

## Consequences

- CC licences are not written for software; they have no patent grant and no source-distribution clause. The product owner accepts this for the non-commercial restriction.
- Contributors accept that their contributions are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; `CONTRIBUTING.md` states this before the first release.
- Dependencies must be compatible with redistribution under these terms (permissive npm licences are).
